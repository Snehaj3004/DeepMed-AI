How it works

You enter one set of vital signs. DeepMed AI generates a 24-step synthetic temporal sequence around those values and sends the sequence to the trained LSTM model.

Pattern options:
Normal Pattern — small natural variations.
Abnormal Pattern (Demo) — gradually changing vital-sign pattern used to demonstrate the model.
Natural Variation — random variations around the supplied values.

This functionality is intended for academic research and decision-support demonstration. It is not a medical diagnosis.
